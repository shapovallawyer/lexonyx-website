Applicability Context

When Malta is genuinely relevant

Malta is not a universal solution. But in specific scenarios, it fits well into the logic of an international structure. Below are the situations where this most often happens.

Holding role in a real group structure

A Maltese holding company may be relevant in structures where shareholder consequences, participation exemption and group distribution logic are genuinely needed — with substance and business rationale, not merely a tax form.

Shareholder-led structures with coherent logic

For structures where tax consequences at shareholder level are assessed together with company-level logic, a Maltese structure may be a justified choice if both levels are properly analysed and supported by appropriate substance.

Service company with real governance and execution

Where a company genuinely carries out activity in Malta, with local management, real operations and governance, a service model may be organic and practically justified.

International structures with practical manageability

For certain international structures, Malta may be a relevant point — where English law, EU membership, practical manageability and business reputation matter together. Not in isolation, but in the context of a real structure.

Specific regulated and credibility-sensitive scenarios

Malta has regulatory infrastructure for financial services and iGaming. For certain regulated contexts, this may be practically significant — provided there is real substance and operational readiness.

Only with real substance and a justified business rationale

A Maltese structure makes sense only where substance is real, management & control is in Malta, and there is a clear explanation of the business logic. This is not optional — without it, the structure is vulnerable to banks, tax authorities and regulators.

Limitations and Risks

Where limitations and risks usually arise

Malta is a mature EU jurisdiction with real legal and regulatory infrastructure. But this does not mean it is suitable for every model. Several areas require careful assessment.

Malta is not a universal answer for tax optimisation

Malta’s tax refund system has created a reputation for a “low effective rate”, but practical implementation requires real substance, compliance and time. The tax benefit is conditional: it depends on the tax residency of the shareholders, the distribution structure and compliance with anti-avoidance rules.

Consequences for shareholders must be assessed together with company logic

The Maltese system operates on two levels: company and shareholder. The tax outcome at shareholder level depends on their residence, CFC rules in their jurisdiction, treaty position and shareholder tax obligations. Analysing the company level without the shareholder level gives an incomplete picture.

Substance and governance must be real, not formal

A Maltese company without real management & control in Malta is a vulnerable position for tax authorities, banks and the MFSA. Board meetings, real directors with authority and operational activity on the ground are not just formalities; they are the basis for defending the structure.

Banking expectations may be stricter than founders expect

Maltese banks operate in a compliance-oriented environment. KYC/AML requirements are strict, and structures with non-standard beneficiary geography or atypical business models may face enhanced due diligence or onboarding difficulties.

Treaty use and cross-border tax position require real business logic

Treaty access through Malta requires compliance with PPT, LOB and anti-avoidance provisions. Malta has actively signed the BEPS convention. Structures using Maltese treaty benefits without real substance and business purpose are vulnerable positions.

Implementation complexity may exceed marketing descriptions

Compliance requirements in Malta are real: statutory reporting, annual accounts, MFSA filings for licensed structures and substance maintenance. These are operating costs that need to be considered in planning. They do not make the structure unworkable — but they exist.

Practical Applicability

Which models Malta is most often relevant for

Malta works well in specific scenarios — where the structure, shareholder logic and real business rationale are aligned.

Holding role in a real group structure

Where a Maltese company performs a real holding function — managing shareholdings and receiving dividends — with real substance, documented management and business rationale.

What to check: substance requirements (directors, board meetings, local presence), participation exemption conditions, shareholder tax residency, CFC exposure and anti-avoidance rules.

Shareholder-led international business with practical applicability

Where shareholder logic — residence, tax position of shareholders and distribution planning — is considered together with the company-level structure, and both levels are aligned.

What to check: tax residency of shareholders and CFC rules in their jurisdictions, treaty position, distribution timing and shareholder tax obligations.

Service company with real management and execution

Service models where delivery and key decisions genuinely take place in Malta — with a local team, documented operations and real governance.

What to check: management & control location, substance, VAT compliance, banking narrative and transfer pricing for intragroup services.

Structures where governance and distribution logic matter together

For structures where Malta adds real value through EU membership, English law and regulatory infrastructure — provided there is substance and clear business logic.

What to check: real vs nominal company functions, documentation trail, operating costs of substance maintenance and banking onboarding readiness.

International structures where consequences for shareholders are aligned

For international structures where company and shareholder levels have been analysed together, substance is real, and there is a clear defensible business logic — not merely a tax form.

What to check: full analysis of the structure at both levels, anti-avoidance exposure, banking narrative, MFSA compliance if regulated, and treaty eligibility.

What matters

What should be understood in advance

Malta is not a universal answer for every structure. The “low effective rate” reputation requires real substance, compliance and analysis at both levels — company and shareholder.
Shareholder logic, company logic and cross-border tax position must be aligned. Analysing only one level without the other gives an incomplete and potentially misleading picture.
Substance and governance must reflect reality. A Maltese structure without real management & control is vulnerable before tax authorities, banks and the regulator.
Jurisdiction selection must correspond to real functions and risks. Malta works where there are real operations, real business logic and alignment across all levels of the structure.
