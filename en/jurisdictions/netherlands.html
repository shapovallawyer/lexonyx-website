Netherlands for investor and group architecture

A Dutch HoldCo can be useful for investment, governance and multi-entity ownership — but institutional investment alone does not make it necessary.

Typical roles

Investor HoldCo, group parent, acquisition platform, cap-table and governance layer, or ownership vehicle for several operating subsidiaries.

Entity Necessity first

If an investor can enter the existing operating company directly and no separate parent function materially improves the structure, a Dutch HoldCo remains optional rather than automatic.

How LEXONYX works

LEXONYX assesses investor requirements, ownership, governance, Entity Necessity and Target State. Dutch corporate, tax, treaty and restructuring conclusions are provided or confirmed by appropriately qualified professionals.
