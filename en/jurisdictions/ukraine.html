ENTITY NECESSITY

A foreign company is not automatically required

International activity, founder residence outside Ukraine or work with EU customers does not by itself mean that a Ukrainian company should be replaced by a foreign HoldCo or that IP should automatically be transferred. Every new entity needs an independent function, Business Purpose and operational logic.

Professional perimeter. LEXONYX performs structural and factual cross-border analysis. Ukrainian-law advice is provided directly within the professional scope of Advokat (Ukraine). Jurisdiction-specific legal, tax and regulatory conclusions in other jurisdictions are provided or confirmed by appropriately qualified professionals.
