1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They are used to make the site work, save your preferences and collect aggregated data about visitor behaviour.

This document explains which cookies the site lexonyx.comuses, for what purpose, and how you can manage your preferences.

We do not use advertising or marketing cookies. We do not share data with advertising networks.

We do not use cookies for automated decision-making, user profiling or evaluation of user behaviour for marketing purposes.

2. Necessary cookies

These cookies support the basic operation of the site. Without them, proper navigation and form functionality are not possible. They are set automatically and do not require your consent.

Cookie Purpose Duration
cookie_consent Remembers your choice regarding analytics cookies Until withdrawn (localStorage)

Technically, your cookie choice is stored in your browser’s localStorage rather than as a cookie: this data is not sent to the server and remains on your device until you clear the site data.

3. Analytics cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the site: which pages are most in demand, where users come from and how long they stay on a page. This helps us improve the site’s content and structure.

Analytics cookies are set only with your consent, which you give through the banner on your first visit to the site.

Cookie Provider Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique visitors 2 years
_ga_* Google Analytics 4 Stores GA4 session state 2 years
_gid Google Analytics Distinguishes visitors within a 24-hour window 24 hours

Google Analytics is configured without data sharing for advertising. IP addresses are anonymised. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent for the use of analytics cookies is collected through a separate banner on the first visit to the site. Analytics cookies are not activated before explicit consent is obtained.

4. Functional cookies

The site does not currently use functional cookies. If they are introduced, for example to store a selected language, this section will be updated before they are activated.

5. Third-party services

Some cookies are set by third-party services that we use to operate the site. We do not control these cookies directly. The providers’ privacy policies are available here:

  • Google Analytics — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Netlify (hosting and forms) — netlify.com/privacy

We do not use third-party advertising networks or tracking pixels.

6. How to manage cookies

You have several ways to manage cookies:

Through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, delete and block cookies. Instructions:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Google Analytics opt-out. You can disable Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out browser add-on.

Important: disabling necessary cookies may affect the proper operation of the site — some functions may stop working correctly.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to withdraw your consent to the use of analytics cookies at any time. To do this, clear the cookie cookie_consent in your browser settings or disable analytics through the Google Analytics Opt-out browser add-on.

You may change your decision at any time by reopening the consent banner or clearing cookies in your browser.

For questions about cookies and personal data processing: privacy@lexonyx.com

More about your rights is available in our Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the set of cookies used or the applicable requirements change. The current version is always available on this page.
