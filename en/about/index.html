LEXONYX PRACTICE

About

LEXONYX is a practice for international business structuring. We begin where an aligned system is needed: structure, tax, governance, banking, presence and the regulatory perimeter.

WHAT KIND OF PRACTICE THIS IS

Not a “bundle of services”, but a way to solve structural matters

The LEXONYX practice is built around one idea: international business cannot be structured properly in fragments. Jurisdiction, beneficial ownership, governance, tax, banking logic and substance must be aligned with one another.

Not registration

Registration is an operation. The practice is the model, logic and alignment of system elements.

Not “one country”

Risks often arise in the connections: team geography → permanent establishment → VAT → banking → governance.

Not “advice in a vacuum”

A solution must withstand review by facts, documents and real operating activity.

HOW WE READ THE MATTER

We read the structure as a system

In international matters, the “right document” does not solve the problem if, in reality, people and management sit elsewhere, and flows cannot be explained to a bank or regulator.

Whether the legal form matches reality

Who makes decisions, where key people are located and how operating activity is structured.

Whether there is an evidence chain

Governance, documents, minutes, contracts and the decision trail — where the position depends on them.

Whether the model can withstand growth

The solution must work during scaling, rather than break at the first bank, hire or new country.

HOW THIS DIFFERS

What the LEXONYX practice is not

Not “the fastest country”

We do not choose a jurisdiction based on one feature if it undermines the logic of the whole model.

Not a promise of outcome

We do not promise an account, a licence or a specific tax outcome. We design defensible logic.

Not a template scheme

The same configuration does not work identically for everyone: people, markets and functions are always different.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

In brief

What is the LEXONYX practice?

LEXONYX is a practice for international business structuring, not a “bundle of services” and not a registration provider. We read a structure as a system: where tax logic, governance, banking usability, presence and the regulatory perimeter must work together. The work begins where a business needs an aligned model, not fragmented actions.

What is the practice not?

The LEXONYX practice is not a registration provider, not the sale of jurisdictions and not a set of template schemes. We do not assemble a task from disconnected actions and we do not guarantee universal solutions. Instead, we read the international model as a system and build an aligned, defensible structure for the specific business situation.

When is this type of practice needed?

This format is needed where the matter affects several elements at once — tax, banking, presence and governance — and their alignment matters. If decisions cannot be made in isolation because they affect one another, the task needs a practice that reads it as a system rather than as a list of separate services.

How is the “About the Practice” section structured?

The section is built around three pages: this introductory page explains the essence of the approach; “How We Work” shows the process and boundaries of the work; “Who We Are” explains the logic of the practice and who we work with. Together, they show how LEXONYX approaches international structures and what to expect from cooperation.
