PRACTICE LED BY

Liudmyla Miroshnychenko

Founder · Lead Adviser

LEXONYX is an independent international advisory practice led by Liudmyla Miroshnychenko, a lawyer with 25 years of experience.

Advokat (Ukraine) since 2004, with a focus on cross-border practice since 2012. Advokat (Ukraine), member of the Munich Bar Association pursuant to §206 BRAO, entitled to advise on Ukrainian and international law. Based in Bavaria, Germany.

Professional focus: international tax law, licensing of fintech companies, banking readiness for non-standard profiles, source-of-funds logic, substance and governance. A structure that cannot explain itself sooner or later becomes indefensible.

Where advice on German or other local law is required, the work is coordinated with local counsel. LEXONYX does not replace local regulated advice where it is mandatory.

CONCEPTUAL AXIS

Model → risks → alignment → documents/processes → readiness

We do not “sell jurisdictions”. We build a system that withstands reality checks: team growth, entry into new markets, bank monitoring and regulatory expectations.

1) First — the factual picture: who does what, where the people are and where sales occur
2) Then — risks: PE/VAT/CFC/banks/regulatory perimeter
3) Then — alignment: company roles, flows, governance and supporting evidence
4) Only then — implementation roadmap and ongoing support
