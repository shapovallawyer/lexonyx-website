Express Risk Check

Rapid diagnostics of an international structure: we identify which risk zones are already active in the model, and whether the matter should move to a full strategic audit.

48–72 hours· Screening without a “full audit”· Go / Fix / Deeper review
Request an Express Assessment Risk Map

Important: this is not a “guarantee of outcome” and not a substitute for a full audit. It is a fast way to understand where the risk is genuinely live — and which next step is defensible on the facts.

WHEN TO USE

When an express-check is the right format

Not every situation requires a full structural audit. Sometimes the first task is to understand quickly where there is an active risk, and where the question looks more serious than it actually is.

Account opening or onboarding

It is necessary to understand quickly whether the structure contains something that will trigger questions from a bank or PSP.

Change of tax residency or hiring in a new country

The model is changing — a first-pass analysis of the consequences is needed before the decision.

Investor / DD / M&A

It is necessary to understand which points will fall into the focus of due diligence before it begins.

There is a sense that “something is not right”

The structure works, but it is unclear exactly where the weak point is and whether it is systemic.

WHAT TO PREPARE

What to prepare for an express assessment

Facts and a diagram are sufficient. Sensitive documents are requested only where necessary and after an NDA/engagement.

1) List of companies + roles (HoldCo/OpCo/ServiceCo, etc.)
2) Geography of key persons and the team (employee/contractor/EOR)
3) Sales markets and model (B2B/B2C/SaaS/marketplace/fintech)
4) Whether there are warehouses/fulfilment/marketplaces (VAT/OSS/IOSS)
5) Intragroup flows (if any): services/royalties/loans/dividends
6) Bank/PSP questions (if already received) — briefly, without attachments

SCOPE

What is reviewed in the express format

The focus is not a full architectural redesign, but the “visibility zones” where structural stress most often arises.

PE · PEOPLE

PE profile

Whether there are roles/authorities/functions that may create taxable presence.

TAX RESIDENCY

Management & control

Where decisions are actually made and whether this matches the stated corporate logic.

BANKING

banking readiness

Explainability of ownership/flows/source of funds/source of wealth narrative and obvious red flags.

VAT

VAT triggers

Warehouses, marketplaces, fulfilment, fixed establishment and their link with the sales model.

CFC · FOUNDER

Personal tax risk

Owner tax residency, CFC exposure, and conflict between ownership and management logic.

SUBSTANCE

substance reality check

Whether there is governance, a decision trail and economic reality to support the stated structure.

BOUNDARY

How an express-check differs from a strategic audit

An express-check shows where active risk is already visible now
A strategic audit is required when the question affects several structural layers at once
An express-check helps make the decision: go / fix locally / detailed review
If the model needs to be redesigned — this is already a strategic audit format
NOT A FIT

The format is not appropriate if a “guaranteed account/licence/tax outcome” is expected, or if the request is connected with sanctions/AML circumvention or evasion.

FAQ

Briefly, to the point

What is an express risk check?

It is a rapid diagnosis of an international structure: we identify which risk zones are already active in the model and whether the matter should move to a full strategic audit. The format answers the question “where is the issue already live”, without replacing a deep analysis, but giving a clear view of priorities and urgency within a short period.

When is an express-check the right format?

An express-check is appropriate when the state of the model must be understood quickly: before a transaction, growth, market entry or the first questions from a bank. It is useful where there is a sense that the structure is diverging from reality, but it is not yet clear how serious this is or where the review should start.

How does an express-check differ from a strategic audit?

An express-check is a rapid review of active risk zones and a decision on whether a full review is required. A strategic audit is a comprehensive analysis of the entire model as a system within one perimeter. The express format answers “is there a problem and where”; the audit answers “how should the structure be reassembled so that it is defensible and robust”.

What should be prepared and what will I receive?

For an express assessment, a general description of the model is sufficient: group structure, jurisdictions, ownership, key operations and current questions. The output is a map of active risk zones, a view of priorities and a clear recommendation — whether targeted fixes are sufficient or whether a full strategic audit of the model is required.
