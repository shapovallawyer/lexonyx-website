HOW TO START

Where the work begins

The work almost never begins with a large project. Usually it starts with a short diagnostic that shows where the structure diverges from reality. From there the scope grows with the task: from a one-off review to an ongoing international legal function.

FOUR LEVELS

Scope grows with the task

One entry point does not commit you to the rest. You can stop at the diagnostic, move on to a project, or build ongoing support — depending on what is needed.

01 · Diagnostic

A short review: where structure, banks and tax diverge from reality, and what is critical first. For those who want to understand the picture before a large project. Format — a one-off diagnostic or express risk review; usually 1–2 weeks. Express Risk Review →

02 · Project

A target model and its implementation: structure, substance, governance, banking readiness. For those whose task is already clear and who need a result, not only a diagnosis. Format — a project; timing depends on scope. Strategic Structure Audit →

03 · Ongoing support

An external international legal function: ongoing access to cross-border advisory without hiring in-house. For growing groups where questions arise regularly. Format — a retainer with tiers of access. External Legal Function →

04 · Coordinating jurisdictions

Several jurisdictions and advisers under a single coordination: project management, an agreed level of response, private capital and family office. For complex international structures. Format — enterprise / SLA. Private Capital and Family Office →

SCOPE AND COST

Honest about how scope is determined

There is no public price list by design. The cost of international work depends too much on the structure, the number of jurisdictions and the nature of the task to be reduced honestly to a line in a table.

  • Diagnostic — a low threshold: understand the picture without a large commitment.
  • Project — scope is set by the target model and the number of jurisdictions.
  • Ongoing support — the tier of access and cadence match the intensity of matters.
  • Coordination — depends on the number of jurisdictions, advisers and the level of response.

Scope and cost are set after an initial qualification of the request. Where a local licence or opinion is required, the work is carried out in coordination with qualified local counsel. We do not guarantee the opening of an account, the grant of a licence or a particular outcome before banks and regulators.

IN SHORT

Frequently asked

How much does it cost?

There is no public price list by design: scope and cost depend on the structure and the task and are set after an initial qualification of the request. The diagnostic is a low-threshold entry point to understand the picture without a large commitment.

Do we have to start with a diagnostic?

No, but it is usually the least expensive way to make sure a larger project addresses the right task and is not built around the wrong hypothesis.

Do you guarantee an outcome?

No. We are responsible for the quality of the analysis and preparation. Decisions by banks and regulators are not within our control, and we do not promise them.
