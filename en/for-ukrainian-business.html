UKRAINIAN CONTEXT · EU · UK · UAE

Ukrainian Owners and Businesses in Europe

A Ukrainian beneficial owner, a foreign company and a third country of actual activity or residence form a distinct configuration with its own logic. We build it so that it can be explained to a bank, a tax authority, a notary, an investor and a regulator.

Request a review About the practice
Residency and CFC· CRS· Place of management· Source of Funds

Why this is a distinct configuration

Ukrainian businesses often became international by circumstance rather than by plan: the founder in one country, the team in another, clients in a third and accounts in a fourth. The country of incorporation, the source of income and the owner's residency stop coinciding — and it is precisely that divergence which a bank and a tax authority test.

The practice is led by an advocate of Ukraine based in Germany: the Ukrainian side is understood from within, the European side from where the practice works.

THE OWNER'S TAX AND REPORTING PERIMETER

What we examine on the owner's side

The key question is not "where to register a company" but how the beneficial owner's residency, the country of incorporation and the place where decisions are actually taken relate to one another.

Personal tax residency

Where the owner becomes resident, how that status is determined and what changes when the family moves.

Ukrainian CFC rules

The scope of control, the obligations of a controlling person, reporting and the link to profit distribution.

CRS and financial accounts

How the automatic exchange of account information relates to the ownership structure and reporting.

Place of management

Where decisions on the foreign company are actually taken and whether this is evidenced in documents.

PE-risk

Whether people, authority or negotiations create a permanent establishment in another country.

Receiving dividends

The logic of distributing profit to the owner: withholding tax, treaties and proof of entitlement to the income.
