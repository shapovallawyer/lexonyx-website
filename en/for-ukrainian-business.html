UKRAINIAN CONTEXT · EU · UK · UAE

For Ukrainian Entrepreneurs and Investors in Europe

LEXONYX helps business owners, investors and founders with a Ukrainian context build international structures that can be explained and defended before a bank, investor, tax authority, notary, regulator or counterparty. We work with situations where the business, assets, team or capital are connected with different jurisdictions — Ukraine, Germany, EU, UK, UAE.

Request a Review About

Why this context matters

Ukrainian business has become genuinely international in recent years — often not by design, but by circumstance. The founder is in one country, the team in another, clients in a third, accounts in a fourth. This reality creates specific legal and banking tasks that cannot be solved by registering yet another company.

The LEXONYX practice is led by a lawyer with a Ukrainian professional background and a German base — this context is understood from within, not from the outside.
