ACCESSIBILITY

Accessibility Statement

LEXONYX aims to make its website accessible to the widest possible range of users, including people with disabilities.

The standard we aim to meet

We follow the recommendations of WCAG 2.2 Level AA and the technical standard EN 301 549, which underpins the European Accessibility Act. These frameworks define how to make digital content perceivable, operable, understandable and robust.

What has already been done

  • Text contrast meets the WCAG AA threshold (at least 4.5:1 for body text).
  • All interactive elements are keyboard-accessible and have a visible focus state.
  • Animations are disabled when the system “reduced motion” setting is enabled.
  • Semantic markup and ARIA attributes are used for navigation, forms and buttons.
  • A “Skip to main content” link is provided to bypass navigation.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website, please let us know — we will try to address it within a reasonable timeframe. Write to us via the contact page, specifying the page and the nature of the issue.

Status

The site is undergoing continuous accessibility improvement. This statement will be updated as the website develops.
