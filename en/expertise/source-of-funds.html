SOURCE OF FUNDS · SOURCE OF WEALTH

A Source-of-Funds Narrative That Can Withstand Scrutiny

When a bank, notary or investor asks for an explanation of the origin of funds, a single document is rarely enough. We prepare a clear, document-backed capital narrative that aligns with your structure, tax logic and real flows.

Request a Review Banking Readiness

The problem is not the document. It is the logic.

Clients often think the bank needs a “certificate”. In practice, the reviewer reconstructs the full picture: how the capital was generated, how it connects to the business, why the flows look the way they do and whether this aligns with the tax history. Disconnected documents without a single logic create more questions than answers.

Source of funds is not a folder of documents. It is a narrative that must be consistent, verifiable and aligned with reality.

WHEN THIS IS NEEDED

Typical Situations

Account Opening

A bank or EMI requests source of funds and an explanation of the business model during onboarding.

Property Purchase

A notary or bank requires evidence of the origin of funds for the transaction.

Major Transfer

A transaction falls outside the usual profile and becomes subject to enhanced review.

Investment or Transaction

An investor, fund or counterparty conducts due diligence on the source of capital.

Capital Relocation

Funds move between jurisdictions and require an explainable history.

Review of a Past Period

The bank asks questions about the origin of funds received earlier.

WHAT WE DO

How the Work Is Structured

Reconstruction of Capital History

We reconstruct how and from what the capital was formed — sequentially and by period.

Document Map

We determine which documents support each element of the narrative and where the gaps are.

Consistency Review

We test the narrative against the structure, tax logic and real flows so that the position does not contradict itself.

Weakness Analysis

We identify the points that will raise questions from the bank or notary before the reviewer asks them.

Explanatory Position

We prepare a coherent written explanation of the origin of funds — in the language of the reviewer.

Action Plan

Where documents are missing, we define what to collect, in what order and how to close the gaps.

LIMITS

What is not sufficient

Source of funds is not a set of documents but a provable narrative. On their own, these do not close the question:

  • A single bank statement without context
  • An income certificate with no history of how the capital was built
  • A set of documents with no timeline — it is unclear what followed what
  • An explanation letter that does not match the tax and business logic
  • Documents that do not explain ownership and control
RESULT

What You Receive

  • A structured source-of-funds narrative — logical and verifiable.
  • A list of supporting documents — what is available and what is missing.
  • A map of weak points — where the position is vulnerable and how to strengthen it.
  • An explanatory position — ready to present to a bank, notary or investor.

IN BRIEF

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between source of funds and source of wealth?

Source of funds is the origin of the specific funds used in a transaction. Source of wealth is the origin of the client’s overall capital and assets. Banks and notaries often review both levels.

When is source of funds preparation needed?

When a bank, notary, broker or investor requests an explanation of the origin of funds: for account opening, a property purchase, a major transfer, an investment or a transaction.

Does this guarantee bank approval?

No. We prepare a clear and document-backed position, but the decision is made by the bank or regulator. LEXONYX does not guarantee any specific result.

Need to explain the origin of funds in a way that leaves no material gaps?

Describe the situation: the bank, notary or investor, the transaction and the request received. We will identify what needs to be prepared.
