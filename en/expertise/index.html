EXPERTISE SYSTEM

Expertise for the International Business Model

An international structure is not one country and not one document. It is an aligned system in which company roles, governance, tax, the bank and the regulatory perimeter work together.

Request a Structure Review View the System
7 elements · Architecture of risk and robustness · Aligned model
Why this matters

Errors arise not within one element, but in the gaps between them

A business may choose a strong jurisdiction and still end up with a weak model: where management in fact takes place in another country, the team creates PE-risk, the bank does not understand the economic logic, and the regulatory function does not match the group structure.

One question rarely remains one question

A residency question almost always touches CFC exposure, team composition, the contractual model and governance.

The bank reads the structure more broadly than the documents

Customer due diligence assesses not only the file pack, but also the overall coherence of the business model.

The regulator looks at the real function

Licensing, substance and the allocation of control must align with how the business actually operates.

TYPICAL TRIGGERS

When it makes sense to start through the Expertise hub

A new country or a new company

The question is not only where to register, but how that choice will affect control, tax, banks and regulation.

Founder or team relocation

People geography changes, raising questions around residency, PE-risk, substance and governance.

A request from a bank or payment partner

In practice, the review tests the coherence of the model: ownership, flows, documents and economic logic.

A licensing plan

The regulatory perimeter, governance, company roles and practical viability of the model need to be checked in advance.

Entry into e-commerce or EU sales

VAT logic, warehousing structure, contractual chain and operating model begin to work together.

Investor or due diligence

A defensible model is needed, not a set of local solutions without a common architectural logic.

FAQ

In brief

What is the LEXONYX Expertise hub?

The Expertise hub is a system of seven interconnected elements of the international model: group structure, tax residency and CFC, VAT, PE-risk, banking readiness, regulatory architecture, and substance with governance. They are not treated as a list of separate services, but as one aligned architecture in which each element affects the others.

Why cannot the expertise elements be analysed separately?

Because errors arise not in one element, but in the gaps between them. A change in residency affects PE-risk; that, in turn, affects VAT and banking readability. If the elements are analysed in isolation, conflicts at the interfaces remain invisible. The hub brings them into one perimeter so the model is tested as an integrated system.

Why is group structuring the foundation of the model?

Group structuring defines company roles, ownership logic, the allocation of functions and flows — the basis on which all other elements rest. Residency, VAT, PE-risk and banking readiness test and reinforce that basis. If the foundation is incoherent, the other elements inherit its weaknesses, which is why the review usually starts here.

When does it make sense to start through the Expertise hub?

The hub is the logical entry point where the task touches several elements at once or the starting point is unclear: the model is becoming more complex, the team is growing, a transaction is being prepared or a market entry is planned. The hub helps read the system as a whole and identify which element needs attention first and how it connects to the rest.

What should be prepared for an initial architectural review?

A map of reality is enough: group structure and ownership, where the owners, directors and team are located, how contractual and money flows work, which jurisdictions and banks are involved, and which questions have already arisen. A perfectly assembled document pack is not needed at the start — what matters is a clear picture of how the model works.
