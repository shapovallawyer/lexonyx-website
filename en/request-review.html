WHAT TO PREPARE

Facts and a structure diagram are enough — no “sensitive” files

If some data is unavailable, provide an approximate answer. For an initial review, the following is usually enough:

1) List of group companies + roles (HoldCo/OpCo/ServiceCo, etc.)
2) Where key people and the team are located (countries, format: employee/contractor/EOR)
3) Sales markets + model (B2B/B2C/SaaS/marketplace/fintech)
4) Whether there are warehouses/fulfillment/marketplaces (for VAT/OSS/IOSS)
5) Whether there is a “regulated perimeter” (payments/crypto/custody, etc.)
6) Any bank/PSP questions, if already received — briefly, without attachments

FAQ

A brief note on the process and liability boundaries.

How long does it take to complete?

Usually 5–8 minutes. If exact data is unavailable, provide an approximate answer; we will clarify the details.

Is an NDA needed?

If sensitive documents or contracts need to be discussed, yes — we will propose an NDA before exchanging materials.

Do you guarantee an account/licence/tax outcome?

No. We design the architecture and evidentiary basis, but decisions are made by banks, regulators and tax authorities.

When will you respond?

After initial qualification. If the input is complete, usually within 1–2 business days.
