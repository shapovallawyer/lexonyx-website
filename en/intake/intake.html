How we work with compliance

Legality and defensibility

We design the structure and evidence base (governance, substance, contracts, narrative) within the law and applicable rules.

No outcome guarantees

We do not guarantee a bank account, licence or specific tax outcome. Decisions are made by banks, regulators and tax authorities.

Sanctions and AML

Requests with indicators of sanctions breaches, AML violations or concealment of a beneficial owner are rejected.

Confidentiality

Sensitive documents only after an NDA / engagement letter. At the intake stage, facts and the structure are enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to complete the form?

Usually 5–7 minutes. If exact data is not available, provide an estimate — we will clarify details later.

Is an NDA needed?

If sensitive documents or contracts need to be discussed, yes — we will propose an NDA before exchanging materials.

Do you guarantee a bank account, licence or tax outcome?

No. We prepare the architecture and evidence base to make the process more predictable, but the final decision always remains with the bank, regulator or tax authority.

What happens after the form is submitted?

We will review the inputs, clarify 3–5 questions if needed, and propose the work format and scope. Response time is usually 1–2 business days.

Which models do you not support?

We do not work with requests related to sanctions evasion, concealment of beneficial ownership, tax evasion or the creation of formal structures without a real operating basis.
