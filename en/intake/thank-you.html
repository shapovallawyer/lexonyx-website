Process After Form Submission

We work within the law and compliance. We do not promise an outcome — account, licence or tax result — but design the architecture and evidence base so that the model is explainable and defendable.

1) Initial Qualification

We review the inputs, identify key risk points and determine the “right entry point”.

2) Clarification, if Needed

We clarify 3–5 questions: geography, company roles, flows, team, VAT, bank and regulatory matters.

3) Format and Scope

We propose a format: risk scan / project / retainer, plus timing guidelines and the scope of deliverables.
