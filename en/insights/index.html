FAQ

Briefly, to the point

What is the “Insights” section at LEXONYX?

Insights are the intellectual layer of the LEXONYX practice. This is not a collection of universal schemes, but a way of thinking: how to read an international structure and where to look for gaps between ownership, management and tax logic. The section consists of three formats — briefings, analyses and tools — and is based on the same system as the expertise section.

Which formats does the section include?

There are three formats: briefings — short analytical updates on what affects international structures; analyses — deep structural cases on group architecture and their tax consequences; tools — practical checklists for an initial assessment of the model. Together, they show context, logic and applied steps.

Do the materials replace personalised analysis?

No. The materials show a way of thinking and typical patterns, but they do not replace a review of a specific structure. Every international model is unique in its combination of jurisdictions, ownership, functions and flows. Insights help identify where to look for risks, but a decision on a specific situation requires personalised structural analysis.

What are the insights based on?

Insights are based on the same system as the expertise section: eight interconnected elements of an international model — group structure, banking readiness, source of funds, tax residency, PE-risk, VAT, substance and regulatory architecture. This means the section’s analytics are aligned with practice and show the real logic of working with structures, rather than abstract schemes.
