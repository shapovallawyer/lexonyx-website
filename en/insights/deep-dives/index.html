FAQ

Briefly, to the point

What are structural analyses?

Analyses are deep cases of international models: the logic of building a group, tax consequences, substance, PE-risk, VAT and banking readiness. They show not universal schemes, but a way of thinking: how the elements of a model are connected and why a structure works or starts to diverge from reality.

Which topics are analysed?

Analyses include the alignment of HoldCo/OpCo/ServiceCo with the real operating model, VAT architecture for e-commerce with OSS, IOSS and fixed establishment, and preparing a structure for banking onboarding. Each case shows the logic of building the model and the typical points where the model most often requires review.

How does an analysis differ from a briefing?

A briefing is a short signal about a change in context. An analysis is a detailed case with the full logic of building a model and its consequences for tax, presence and the bank. Analyses provide depth of understanding of how a resilient structure is assembled, while briefings keep track of changes in the external environment.

Are the analyses applicable to my structure?

Analyses show patterns and logic applicable to many models, but they do not replace analysis of your specific structure. They help you understand how to think about your own architecture and which questions to ask. Assessing your model with its specific jurisdictions and flows is possible only through a personalised review.
