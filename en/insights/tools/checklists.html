Checklists

Applied screening tools for assessing an international structure: tax risks, PE, VAT, banking readiness and regulatory alignment.

Home → Analytics → Playbooks → PE Risk

Checklists help assess the level of risk and identify areas requiring structural analysis. They do not replace legal advice and are not a ready-made solution.

Structuring · Governance

Cross-Border Structuring Screening

  • Does the structure match the actual allocation of functions?
  • Where are key management decisions made?
  • Is the group’s economic rationale documented?
  • Is the tax model aligned with governance?
Complete an extended review →
PE · People · Remote Teams

PE-Risk & People Footprint Checklist

  • Are there employees or agents in other jurisdictions?
  • Who conducts negotiations and signs contracts?
  • Where is effective management carried out?
  • Could a dependent agent PE arise?
Assess PE-risk →
VAT · E-commerce

VAT & Fixed Establishment Screening

  • Are warehouses or fulfillment centres used in the EU?
  • Is there personnel connected with the supply of goods?
  • Is the OSS regime sufficient?
  • Do local VAT obligations arise?
Check the VAT model →
Banking · KYC · Regulatory

Banking Readiness Checklist

  • Is the ownership structure consistent?
  • Is the source of funds documented?
  • Is there a narrative explaining the economic rationale?
  • Does governance meet bank expectations?
Check readiness →

What next?

If a checklist produces several “red zones”, the structure requires systemic analysis. International models rarely have only one issue: tax, governance, banking and regulatory logic are interconnected.
