Checklist

Substance Readiness

Review of real presence, management and documentation of the structure.

Home → Analyses → Checklists → Substance

1. Management

  • Where are the directors located?
  • Where are meetings held?
  • Is there a documented governance structure?

2. People and Functions

  • Do the functions match the stated role of the company?
  • Is there evidence that the functions are actually performed?
  • Does the profit allocation correspond to the functions and risks?

3. Documentation

  • Is there transfer pricing documentation?
  • Do the contracts reflect the factual model?
  • Is the banking narrative aligned?
