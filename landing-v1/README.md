## Brand direction retained

- teal/navy foundation
- restrained gold accent
- warm cream sections
- editorial serif + clean sans-serif
- architectural, quiet, premium visual language
- founder-led practice

## Positioning reflected in this prototype

Lexonyx leads cross-border corporate structuring matters, performs factual/structural analysis and project coordination, provides Ukrainian-law advice within the founder's professional scope, and works with appropriately qualified local professionals for jurisdiction-specific legal and tax conclusions outside that scope.
