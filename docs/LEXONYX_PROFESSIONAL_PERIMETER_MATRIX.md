# LEXONYX Professional Perimeter Matrix

Version: 1.0  
Status: internal compliance standard  
Purpose: define what LEXONYX may deliver directly, what may be led and coordinated by LEXONYX with specialist confirmation, and what must remain owned by an appropriately qualified local legal/tax/regulatory professional.

## 1. Core operating rule

LEXONYX does not present itself as a universal provider of legal or tax advice across jurisdictions.

LEXONYX may directly provide Ukrainian-law legal services within the professional scope of Advokat (Ukraine) and may independently perform factual, structural, operational, evidence, banking-readiness and project-coordination work that does not itself constitute a reserved jurisdiction-specific legal or tax conclusion.

Where a matter requires a conclusion under German or other foreign law, taxation or regulated local rules, LEXONYX may identify the issue, structure the facts, define the specialist question, coordinate the workstream and integrate the confirmed conclusion into the overall Target State. The jurisdiction-specific regulated conclusion must be attributable to the appropriately qualified professional.

## 2. Decision labels

### LEXONYX ONLY
Work LEXONYX may own and deliver directly, provided it does not cross into a reserved foreign-law or tax conclusion.

### LEXONYX + SPECIALIST
LEXONYX may lead the matter, perform the structural/factual layer, coordinate the workstream and issue an integrated memorandum, but the relevant jurisdiction-specific conclusion must be provided or confirmed by an appropriately qualified specialist.

### SPECIALIST-OWNED CONCLUSION
The substantive legal/tax/regulatory conclusion must be owned by the appropriately qualified specialist. LEXONYX may coordinate and integrate it but must not present the conclusion as its own independent foreign-law/tax opinion.

## 4. Website wording rule

### Approved master wording

> LEXONYX conducts structural and factual analysis of cross-border matters, including ownership, functions, governance, operational reality, banking, evidence and implementation. Ukrainian-law advice is provided directly by Advokat (Ukraine). Where a project requires legal, tax or regulatory conclusions under another jurisdiction, LEXONYX coordinates appropriately qualified professionals and integrates their confirmed conclusions into the overall architecture of the project.

### Short version

> Jurisdiction-specific legal, tax and regulatory conclusions are provided or confirmed by appropriately qualified professionals where required.

## 5. Prohibited / high-risk standalone claims

Do not market LEXONYX as independently providing, unless and until the relevant professional authority exists:

- German legal advice;
- German tax advice;
- EU-wide legal advice as a generic product;
- UK / UAE / US / Cyprus / Netherlands / other foreign-law legal opinions;
- standalone foreign-tax opinions;
- MiCA or other regulated legal opinions as LEXONYX-only work;
- local representation, filings or licensing work reserved to local professionals.

## 6. Red Flag vs Finding rule

LEXONYX may independently identify a potential PE, CFC, VAT, residence, licensing, sanctions, AML or other legal/tax/regulatory interface as a red flag based on facts and operating reality.

A red flag must not be converted into a definitive jurisdiction-specific legal/tax finding unless the relevant conclusion is within LEXONYX's own professional scope or has been confirmed by the appropriately qualified specialist.

## 7. Client-delivery rule

For mixed-jurisdiction matters, the integrated memorandum should distinguish:

1. Matter Facts / Evidence;
2. LEXONYX structural findings;
3. jurisdiction-specific specialist conclusions;
4. assumptions and unresolved questions;
5. Target State and implementation dependencies.

The integrated recommendation may be issued by LEXONYX as project lead, provided the source and ownership of specialist conclusions remain transparent.
