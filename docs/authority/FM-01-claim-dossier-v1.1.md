## Source hierarchy rules

1. **German corporate tax residence:** use §1 KStG + §10 AO as the primary statutory basis. Do **not** use the BMF letter of 18 June 2026 as a substitute for those statutes.
2. **German management / home-office / PE detail:** use the BMF letter of 18 June 2026 to explain Geschäftsleitungsbetriebsstätte, laufende Geschäftsführung / Tagesgeschäft, extraordinary decisions and treaty home-office analysis.
3. **Treaty propositions:** verify the actual bilateral treaty first, then any protocol, MLI matching, reservations/notifications and dates of effect. OECD Model/Commentary and the MLI are analytical authorities, not substitutes for checking the actual treaty relationship.
4. **CFC for an individual founder:** derive the conclusion from the domestic CFC legislation applicable to the individual. ATAD is a minimum CFC framework for corporate taxpayers, not a direct source of personal CFC liability.
5. **OECD 2025 home-office framework:** never present the 50% line as a statutory safe harbour or presumption of PE. Below 50%, a home generally is not a place of business on that fact alone; at 50% or more, a full facts-and-circumstances analysis continues, with commercial reason a prominent consideration. Country positions and the actual treaty/practice must still be checked.
6. **Residence distinction:** domestic-law residence and treaty residence are related but legally distinct questions. Do not write that a treaty tie-breaker automatically extinguishes every domestic-law consequence in the other state.
7. **Payments:** analyse dividends, employment remuneration, directors' fees, interest and related-party fees separately. Do not collapse them into one treaty-flow category.
8. **CRS / AML / KYC:** explain the operational interface, but never describe them as the same legal regime.
9. **Governance:** documents are evidence of governance; they do not substitute for the real management and operating process. Never recommend artificial substance or minutes inconsistent with reality.

## High-risk publication formulations — approved form

### Master thesis

> A founder’s relocation does not automatically relocate the company. But it can change facts on which legal, tax, treaty, banking and governance analyses depend. Personal tax residence is only one layer. The correct process starts by identifying what actually changed and then applying the relevant domestic and treaty rules to each issue separately. Founder relocation should therefore be treated as a cross-border structural event, not merely as a personal tax-residence question.

### German management

> For German domestic-law purposes, the analysis focuses on where the company’s actual ongoing management is carried out, including where management acts forming part of the ordinary conduct of the business are in fact performed.

### OECD 2025 home office

> Working from a home abroad does not automatically create a permanent establishment. The OECD's 2025 Commentary indicates that use below 50% of total working time would generally not, on that fact alone, make the home a place of business; at 50% or more, the analysis does not end — it becomes a full facts-and-circumstances inquiry, with commercial reason a prominent consideration.

### Management from home

> A founder working from home and a founder managing the company from home are not necessarily the same PE fact pattern.

### CFC

> At EU level, ATAD establishes a minimum CFC framework for corporate taxpayers. A founder’s personal CFC exposure must instead be tested under the domestic rules applicable to individuals in the relevant jurisdiction.

### Dual residence / MLI

> Dual residence is not necessarily resolved by choosing the more persuasive management narrative. Under some MLI-modified treaties it becomes a competent-authority question, and treaty relief may be unavailable in the absence of agreement.

### Germany–UAE sidebar

> Before applying a treaty, verify that an applicable income-tax treaty exists. Germany's income-tax treaty with the UAE ceased to apply after 31 December 2021, although separate tax-information exchange arrangements continue.

### Governance

> Corporate documents are evidence of governance. They do not substitute for governance. Board minutes cannot, by themselves, cure a management model that operates differently in practice.
